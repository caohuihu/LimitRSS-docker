# LimitRSS v0.2.6

## 中文说明

### 改进

- **"AI 结果"勾选区始终显示 总结/翻译 两种类型**：之前只列出该文章已生成过的结果，没生成总结就看不到"AI 总结"选项。现在内置文章级类型（AI 总结、AI 翻译）始终可见——已生成的可勾选（默认全勾），未生成的显示提示：先在阅读器顶部点对应 AI 图标生成，再回来保存。

## English

### Improved

- **The "AI results" checkbox area now always shows both Summary and Translation**: previously only already-generated results were listed, so the "AI summary" option was invisible when none had been generated. Built-in article-level types are now always visible — generated ones are checkable (checked by default); missing ones show a hint to generate them in the reader first.
